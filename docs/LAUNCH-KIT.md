## Full description

Uranium Strategy is a burn-to-build strategy game on Solana. Players burn fixed-supply $USR to level rigs across a personal 25-cell reserve. Mining power earns a pro-rata share of a finite, pre-funded reward vault with deterministic weekly halvings. Claim to your wallet or compound rewards into more power. $USR is game utility; it is not ownership of physical uranium, a uranium stock, or an ETF.
